Leaf area from graph paper


Photograph a leaf on 1 cm graph paper, mark a known distance, and the tool counts the leaf's pixels to find its area.

Load a photo

Take the picture from directly above, with the leaf flat on the graph paper.

Tips for a good photo
  • Hold the camera level, straight over the paper. Tilted shots stretch the grid and throw off the area.
  • Flatten the leaf under clear tape or a sheet of glass. Curled edges hide area.
  • Use even light with no shadow from your phone or hand.
  • Keep the leaf away from the paper's edge and other objects.

Set the scale

With Set scale selected, click two points on grid lines a known distance apart. A longer line gives a more accurate result. Drag either end to adjust it.
