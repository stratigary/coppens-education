Chi-Square (χ2) Goodness of Fit Test

χ2 = Σ [(O − E)2 ÷ E]

Where:

  • χ2 = Chi-Square statistic
  • O = Observed frequency (your actual data)
  • E = Expected frequency (your predicted data based on a null hypothesis, e.g., a Punnett square ratio)
  • Σ = Sum of all categories
